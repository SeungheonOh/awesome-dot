# Safe use and publication boundaries

## Start with synthetic or sanitized inputs

The examples in this repository are hypothetical. Do not commit bank exports, personal documents, inboxes, location history, client material, keys or passwords. Supply only the data needed for your project. A private chat and a public repository have different audiences.

## Financial projects

Finance recipes are read-only budgeting, arithmetic, organization and educational scenario analysis. They do not recommend specific investments, claim guaranteed returns, place trades, transfer money, open accounts or collect credentials. Verify source dates, currency, assumptions and calculations. A model is not individualized professional advice. For consequential decisions, seek a qualified professional and use the institution's official interface yourself.

## News and research

Request primary sources where available, dated links, coverage windows, publication dates and uncertainty. Separate verified facts, interpretation and missing evidence. Publication time may differ from the event date. Conflicting sources should remain visible. A lack of search results is not proof that nothing happened. Summarize rather than reproducing copyrighted articles.

## Images, design and identity

Use images, fonts, music and other assets you own or are authorized to use. Do not fabricate evidence, endorsements or real people's actions. Label synthetic or illustrative material when its context could mislead. Review text, symbols and identifying details before publication.

## Scheduling and communications

Set the personal timezone and a bounded cadence. Ask for confirmation of actual setup. Keep reminders private unless a recipient and purpose have been explicitly chosen. A reminder to draft a message does not authorize sending it. A monitor should report relevant results without taking unapproved actions.

## Code, games and 3D

Begin with a local or private prototype and test important failure paths. Avoid collecting personal data unless essential and intentionally designed. Review dependencies and licenses. Do not present a browser simulation as real-world scientific validation. 3D study models are not certified structural, medical, electrical or safety equipment. Check exports in the receiving application before fabrication.

## Before anything leaves your private workspace

1. Identify the exact file, message or site
2. Confirm who can receive or access it
3. Remove unintended personal and confidential data
4. Review any payment, account, legal or permission consequence
5. Verify the actual destination and action result

## Reporting a problem in this repository

Use a repository issue for a content error or unsafe instruction, with a minimal non-sensitive example. Do not upload credentials or personal account material. For a security issue involving a separate service or account, use that service's official reporting route rather than publishing it here.
