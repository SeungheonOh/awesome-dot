# Recipient/access matrix

Item: stable ID, safe canonical link or private reference, owner, type, content revision

Scope: named file or folder and explicitly included children; intended data/audience/capabilities

Evidence: service/account, permission revision, observation time, official behavior references, inspection coverage and limits

| Recipient and verified account | Desired capability | Confirmed routes and origin | Unknown routes | Organization/session constraints | Permission conclusion | Recipient opening evidence | Minimal correction and authority | Readback or remaining step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact resolved account | Requested view/comment/edit/download capability | Grant ID, role, ancestor or group, activation/expiry, evidence reference | Missing membership or permission coverage | Policy result; session mismatch | Confirmed / absent / blocked / unknown, scoped to capability | Observed / reported / failed / untested for this action; item, account, time, revision | Exact item, account, role, authorized / needs decision / none | Current observed state, evidence reference or smallest missing observation |

Repeat a recipient row for each requested capability when entitlement or usability differs. For example, “view confirmed; opening reported” and “download blocked; download untested” must remain separate outcomes. An expired grant contributes no entitlement through that route; evaluate other known or unknown routes before concluding that the recipient has no access.

Keep a separate short list for incidental access found within the authorized inspection. Do not silently promote those accounts into the intended recipient list. For groups, report aggregate scope when individual membership is unnecessary or inaccessible. Do not copy the entire membership directory into this report.

Change record: action, affected item and grant IDs, precise before/after values, authorization reference, receipt status and readback evidence. Distinguish proposed, applied, partially applied and uncertain outcomes.

Completion wording: “The viewer grant for [resolved account] is present on [item] at [revision/time]. [Recipient-side evidence status]. [Remaining constraint, if any].”
