# Cabinet repair quote decision sheet

Condensed result from one fictional rehearsal. Comparison date: **2026-10-01** · Currency: **EUR**. The [input packet](input.md) is reproduced for this public example; its private-output instruction is part of the fictional scenario. All tax rates are supplied fictional quote terms, not statements of law.

## Decision in brief

**No quote establishes a complete, firm, current all-in offer.**

- **A:** full documented scope at a fixed **EUR384.00 including VAT**, with correct arithmetic, but expired September 30. Renewal is missing; no scope or arithmetic defect has been demonstrated ([A](input.md#quote-a-issued-september-15-valid-through-september-30))
- **B:** current within its stated validity, but the **EUR360.00 base excludes required refinishing**. Adding it gives **EUR440.00 of listed amounts**, with tax basis and price firmness unresolved ([B](input.md#quote-b-issued-september-28-valid-through-october-31))
- **C:** current and complete in documented scope, but **EUR396.00 is an estimate**, including an uncapped materials allowance that can move up or down ([C](input.md#quote-c-issued-september-29-valid-through-october-15))

The first useful question is whether A will renew the same complete fixed all-in price with its assumptions stated explicitly. Resolve B's tax/firmness and C's allowance limit before treating either as a firm alternative. These are draft questions; no supplier has been selected or contacted.

| **Net subtotal** | **320.00** |

This matches the stated total: arithmetic discrepancy **EUR0.00**. The fixed price is subject to documented scope and assumptions, which the excerpt does not enumerate. The September 15 quote expired September 30; it is historical price evidence requiring renewal, not a currently confirmed offer ([A](input.md#quote-a-issued-september-15-valid-through-september-30)).

The **EUR96.00 deposit is part of EUR384.00**, leaving EUR288.00 if the offer is renewed unchanged. It is not added again. Optional decorative handles are EUR40.00 net outside the required scope. **If the same 20% VAT applies to that addition**, they add EUR48.00 gross and the optional scenario is EUR432.00; removing them returns it to EUR384.00. Both the optional tax treatment and expired offer require confirmation ([A](input.md#quote-a-issued-september-15-valid-through-september-30)).

### B: listed required amounts, unresolved all-in price

| Component | Listed EUR amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Alignment labor | 260.00 |
| Materials | 100.00 |
| Waste removal | Included |
| **Recalculated base** | **360.00** |

The base agrees with the stated EUR360.00: arithmetic discrepancy **EUR0.00**. EUR440.00 is only a sum of listed amounts, not a verified net, gross or tax-normalized comparison total. Confirm that the components share a tax basis. If all include tax and cover every mandatory charge, the complete total would be EUR440.00; otherwise the packet cannot establish it. Do not import A or C's 20% VAT rate into B ([B](input.md#quote-b-issued-september-28-valid-through-october-31)).

The EUR100.00 deposit is credited toward the accepted price, not added to EUR440.00. The balance is unresolved because the accepted all-in price is unknown. A fixed-price promise and variation terms are not supplied. Valid through October 31 does not by itself mean the price is firm ([B](input.md#quote-b-issued-september-28-valid-through-october-31)).

### C: complete scope, variable materials estimate

| Component | EUR |
| --- | ---: |
| Alignment/materials, net, **already including EUR80.00 allowance** | 200.00 |
| Refinishing, net | 100.00 |
| Waste removal, net | 30.00 |
| **Baseline net subtotal** | **330.00** |
| VAT: supplied 20% of net charges | 66.00 |
| **Estimated all-in total** | **396.00** |

The estimate matches the stated total: arithmetic discrepancy **EUR0.00**. Do not add the EUR80.00 allowance a second time. Let M be actual materials cost in EUR net. Holding the remaining documented charges unchanged ([C](input.md#quote-c-issued-september-29-valid-through-october-15)):

- Final net price = **EUR330.00 + (M − EUR80.00)**
- Final gross price = **EUR396.00 + 1.20 × (M − EUR80.00)**
- M = EUR70.00 / EUR80.00 / EUR90.00 gives EUR384.00 / EUR396.00 / EUR408.00 gross

These are arithmetic scenarios, not forecasts or a guaranteed cap. No allowance cap or final fixed price is supplied. The formula does not price unlisted site work. The **deposit is EUR198.00**, 50% of the estimated EUR396.00, credited against the eventual final price. The baseline balance is EUR198.00; an adjusted balance is final gross price minus EUR198.00. It is not necessarily half of the eventual final price ([C](input.md#quote-c-issued-september-29-valid-through-october-15)).

## Tradeoffs and evidence limits

A's documented fixed price offers the clearest price certainty **if renewed unchanged and its assumptions are acceptable**. C supplies current complete-scope evidence but leaves material-cost uncertainty. B's required-work raw subtotal still needs tax and firmness confirmation. C's EUR396.00 baseline is EUR12.00 above A's expired EUR384.00 offer; that difference is not a guaranteed saving. B's EUR440.00 raw subtotal cannot be ranked as an equivalent all-in total. No weighted score or final supplier ranking is justified by the [supplied priorities and excerpts](input.md).

The [packet](input.md) gives no independently inspected site facts, measurements, quantities, detailed finish/material standards, warranty or cancellation/deposit-refund terms. A's proposed start depends on materials; B and C give no scheduling statement. No firm completion date is established. These omissions are unknowns, not favorable terms or evidence of zero extra charges.

## Prioritized clarification questions

These questions remain drafts, tied to the corresponding source excerpt.

### For A

1. Will you renew alignment, refinishing and waste removal at a **fixed EUR384.00 including VAT and all mandatory charges**? What is the new validity date, and has anything changed?
2. Which site, materials and finish assumptions qualify that price, and what circumstances could change it?
3. When could you commit to start and finish? What are the warranty, cancellation and deposit-refund terms?

Basis: [A's expired fixed offer, deposit and contingent start](input.md#quote-a-issued-september-15-valid-through-september-30). Expiry needs reconfirmation; the stated arithmetic is correct.

### For B

1. Include required refinishing and state one **fixed, complete-scope all-in price**. Do all listed amounts include tax? Is the total EUR440.00, or what tax/mandatory charges must be added?
2. Does the EUR80.00 addition cover all worked surfaces, with waste removal still included? What assumptions or variations could change the price?
3. What start/completion commitments, warranty, cancellation and deposit-refund terms apply?

Basis: [B's mandatory add-on, unknown tax basis and deposit](input.md#quote-b-issued-september-28-valid-through-october-31).

### For C

1. Can you offer a **fixed all-in price or explicit maximum including VAT**, stating its coverage and exceptions?
2. What materials and quantities underpin the EUR80.00 net allowance, how is actual cost evidenced, and how are both decreases and increases passed through? What approval is needed for an overrun, and are other charges variable?
3. Confirm the finish standard, site assumptions, dates, warranty and cancellation/deposit-refund terms, including the EUR198.00 deposit credit.

Basis: [C's variable allowance and estimated deposit](input.md#quote-c-issued-september-29-valid-through-october-15).

The decision remains open. No supplier choice, booking, payment, acceptance or live availability confirmation is established by this comparison.
