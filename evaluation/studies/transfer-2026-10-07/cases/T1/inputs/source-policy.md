# Relay exporter owner policy, snapshot RX-2026-09-30

This is original fictional data. The workload registry and the charges/credits feeds are separate exports for the cost dashboard. Treat ID keys as opaque identifiers; their numbering and spelling carry no extra meaning. Amounts include signed corrections. Credit amounts are already signed and are added, not subtracted again. Do not cap or floor amounts.

Population: workload status is active, started_on >= report_start and < report_end, and posted_at <= as_of. Event eligibility: status is final, effective_on <= the UTC date portion of as_of, and posted_at <= as_of. Event activity is attached to the workload cohort and is not limited to the cohort's start/end interval. Equality at the cutoff is included. A present workload outside the cohort is not an orphan.

An event allocates only when its workload key names an eligible observed workload and its currency matches that workload. All eligible events still belong in the independent feed control comparison. Unknown keys, absent keys, out-of-cohort records, and wrong-currency records are retained as exceptions, with original amounts and units; do not guess their tenant or convert amounts. Eligible wrong-currency events on an eligible workload make that stream's workload total unknown, because an owner must resolve the unit defect.

`coverage` comes from individual workload-stream export acknowledgements. complete certifies that every eligible event for that workload/stream was exported, including a genuinely empty stream. partial means the supplied rows are not the whole stream. An absent acknowledgement gives no completeness evidence. Neither partial nor missing coverage permits a zero total. A complete stream with any same-currency null amount still has an unknown total; preserve the sum of the known amounts only as a known subtotal. Do not treat a complete acknowledgement as evidence that nulls are zero or that a wrong currency was correct.

`population_controls` is an independent registry-owner export for exactly the requested population, by tenant/currency. The expected count and base sum are source controls, not values calculated from this extract. extraction_state records whether the workload page export is complete. An absent control-partition workload cannot be synthesized from the count or base gap. The dogwood control partition is intentionally complete and empty. Report every control partition.

`feed_controls` is an independent settlement-export control for the entire eligible event population in each stream/currency at this cutoff, before workload allocation. It includes null-valued and unallocatable rows. known_subtotal_cents sums only non-null source amounts. It does not certify all values are known. Preserve every control difference as a discrepancy. Differences do not identify the missing rows, their workload, or whether they concern an absent workload or a partial/missing stream. Per-workload complete acknowledgements remain authoritative for those workloads, so do not spread a global feed discrepancy across certified streams. The packet includes all available evidence; no external fetch can repair it.

Registry and feed controls have different populations. Reconcile them separately; never add USD to EUR, or force net cost to equal a feed subtotal. Results apply only to this fictional snapshot.
