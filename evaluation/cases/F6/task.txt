Only refunds whose order_id has no row anywhere in orders are unallocated. Report qualifying unallocated refunds, through as_of inclusively, in a mandatory special channel row __unallocated__, even if there are none. Its order_count, line_row_count, known_gross_minor, incomplete_order_count, complete_zero_order_count and gross_minor are all zero; refund_minor is the qualifying unmatched amount; net_minor is NULL because no cohort allocation is authorized; unallocated_refund_count counts those qualifying rows.
