P1 | Travel and operating expense policy, effective through 2026-09-14 inclusive
Reimburse documented business transportation. Standard taxi/rideshare cap is USD 60.00 per trip unless a written exception names the trip and limit. Meal caps per person per meal, tax included: breakfast USD 15.00, lunch USD 25.00, dinner USD 65.00. Alcohol and its proportional tax are never reimbursable. To remove alcohol, prorate the total receipt tax across food and alcohol according to pre-tax amounts, and round the food-plus-food-tax amount once to cents using ROUND_HALF_UP before applying the cap. Do not round intermediate ratios.
Hotel room base cap is USD 180.00 per night; separately stated mandatory lodging tax remains fully reimbursable even when the room base exceeds the cap. Written lodging exceptions replace the room-base cap for the named stay. A deposit applied to a final bill must not be reimbursed twice.

P2 | Policy amendment, effective for service dates on or after 2026-09-15
Taxi/rideshare standard cap increases to USD 75.00 per trip. Tax-inclusive meal caps become breakfast USD 20.00, lunch USD 30.00, dinner USD 80.00 per person. All other P1 terms remain unchanged. A posting date after September 15 does not change the policy for earlier service.

Rules unchanged in both versions
Employee-paid business charges require an itemized receipt when the claimed amount exceeds USD 25.00 (or foreign equivalent). At or below USD 25.00, a settled statement entry plus a specific business purpose is sufficient. Missing required evidence goes on hold, not automatic rejection. Team meals require the attendee list/count and business purpose; do not infer attendees from a meeting invite. Ordinary individual meals do not require an attendee list. A supported receipt amount and settled amount differing by more than USD 1.00 requires clarification before reimbursement unless an explicit deposit, refund, or other supplied source explains it. Supported final invoices supersede estimates.
Foreign-currency employee-card transactions use the settled USD charge, not an internet exchange rate. For applying a USD cap to a foreign meal, use the settled USD amount when no excluded line items exist. Foreign cash claims require the organization's approved exchange-rate record; without one, hold the original currency and leave USD valuation unknown. Do not infer a cash rate from another transaction.
Software purchases require a named written approval. Personal purchases are ineligible. Settled refunds offset the original claim even if a claim was filed before the credit appeared. A business-related cancellation fee is reimbursable only when the reason and fee are approved in writing. Company-card expenses are recorded as business cost when supported but never paid to the employee. Reuse of the same receipt is not an additional expense.
