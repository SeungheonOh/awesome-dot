# Start here

You can use this repository without cloning it. Pick one recipe, copy its main prompt into dot, replace the placeholders, and provide the requested inputs.

## 1. Choose a result, not a feature

Good first goals are small and inspectable: a transparent-background image, a one-page research brief, one working game level, or one reminder with an explicit timezone. Avoid starting with “run my entire life” or “build a production platform.”

Use the [catalog](CATALOG.md) to choose a category and difficulty. The suggested timebox is a planning budget for your first session, not a delivery-time estimate.

## 2. Check the access you actually have

Ask dot which parts it can complete now. You may need to provide a file, connect an app, make a computer available, or handle a protected step yourself. Chat access, app access and computer access are different. Do not assume an open app is connected.

Use the [capability guide](docs/CAPABILITIES.md). If an integration is unavailable, a draft based on pasted or uploaded material can still be valuable. Do not share passwords or account tokens in chat.

## 3. Supply the minimum useful inputs

Replace every bracketed placeholder in the recipe. Prefer a small fictional example or a sanitized sample for the first pass. For images, use material you own or are authorized to edit. For news, specify topic, region, time range and source expectations. For reminders, specify your timezone, timing and when the work should stop.

## 4. Ask for the smallest complete version

Paste the main prompt. Let dot surface essential questions and access limits. Ask it to produce one useful artifact before expanding the scope. Drafting, running, exporting, scheduling and publishing are different milestones.

## 5. Check the result

Walk through the recipe's acceptance checks. Open the actual file. Reset the game. Try an empty input. Inspect image edges. Verify a quoted source and its date. Confirm the actual reminder schedule. If dot cannot run a check, it should mark it unverified and give you a practical way to finish it.

## 6. Iterate with a purpose

Use the three follow-up prompts in order or pick the one that addresses your biggest problem. Change one important dimension at a time. Keep a [run log](templates/RUN-LOG.md) so you know which artifact and conditions you tested.

## 7. Decide what happens next

Only share, deploy, send invitations, make purchases or change account settings when you mean to. Review the destination, audience and data involved. A successful private prototype does not establish that public deployment is safe or authorized.

### Four honest outcomes

- **Draft ready:** you have something useful to inspect
- **Checked locally:** specific checks passed in a stated environment
- **Needs access or your action:** a named dependency prevents the next step
- **Scheduled or published:** the actual service confirms the action, destination and settings

None of those outcomes should be inferred from a confident-looking message alone.
