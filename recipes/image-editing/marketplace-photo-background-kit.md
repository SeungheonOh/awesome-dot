# Marketplace Photo Background Kit

Create a consistent set of product-photo backgrounds without concealing material product defects.

## Scenario

You are preparing a listing for a secondhand desk lamp photographed against several distracting backgrounds. The images should look consistent without making the item appear new or changing its size. You need a private review kit before deciding what to upload to the marketplace.

## Inputs to prepare

- Authorized photos of the item from at least three useful angles
- The intended listing platform and its current image requirements, if known
- Preferred background colors and desired crop ratios
- A list of scratches, labels, scale references, and accessories that must remain visible

## Copy this prompt into dot

```text
dot, turn [AUTHORIZED PRODUCT PHOTOS] into a consistent background kit for a draft listing on [PLATFORM]. Use [BACKGROUND COLORS] and [CROP RATIOS]. First inspect the photos and identify boundaries, transparent surfaces, cables, labels, and shadows that may make background replacement difficult. Ask for a better source photo when the product edge is unclear rather than inventing missing parts.

With available image tools, create a neutral-background treatment and one contextual treatment using the same product angle. Preserve visible wear, included accessories, branding, and proportions as closely as the tools allow; never remove a defect or add an item that is not for sale. Include an unedited reference in the review sheet. Do not claim exact geometry or lettering preservation after a generative edit.

Deliver labeled candidate images, crop previews, and a concise quality checklist. Inspect edge halos, disconnected cables, inconsistent shadows, and the smallest readable product label. Report source-resolution limits and any feature that drifted. If platform requirements were supplied, check against them; otherwise mark them unverified. Keep the kit private for my review and do not upload images, create a listing, or imply the contextual scene shows actual included furnishings without approval.
```

## Iterate with a purpose

### 1. Inspect fine edges

```text
Review cable edges, openings, and translucent parts against the source. Replace any unreliable edit with an explicit reshoot recommendation.
```

### 2. Build listing sequence

```text
Arrange the approved images into a proposed listing sequence that shows overall condition, scale, controls, and wear without uploading it.
```

### 3. Test small previews

```text
Create small thumbnail previews and identify any crop that hides a defect, accessory, or important product feature.
```

## Expected deliverables

- Neutral and contextual background candidates
- Source-to-candidate review sheet
- Crop and thumbnail previews
- Quality and reshoot checklist

## Acceptance checks

- All listed accessories match the source photos
- Visible wear is neither removed nor disguised
- Fine cables and openings are checked for edge artifacts
- A reflective or translucent edge is flagged if unreliable
- Contextual objects are distinguished from items included in the sale
- Image dimensions and crop ratios are recorded

## Access, privacy and stop conditions

- Image availability and export formats vary by account and environment
- Product geometry and label text require visual verification after editing
- No listing creation, upload, or buyer communication is authorized
- Contextual imagery must not misrepresent condition or included items

## Two possible extensions

- Draft factual captions from user-confirmed specifications
- Create a reusable shooting checklist for future listings
